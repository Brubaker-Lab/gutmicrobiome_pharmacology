# Supplementary Tables

This directory contains the supplementary tables associated with the manuscript:

## A Systems Biology Framework Links Gut Microbiome Metabolites to Regorafenib-Associated Transcriptional Programs in Colorectal Cancer

---

## Supplementary Table S1a

**File:** `Table S1a - Top regorafenib-concordant metabolites.csv`

**Title:** Top regorafenib-concordant metabolites.

**Description:** Metabolites with positive transcriptional concordance with the LINCS regorafenib perturbation profile that met the candidate-prioritization criteria across the unadjusted, sex-adjusted, and disease-adjusted analyses.

## Supplementary Table S3

**File:** `Table S3 - Beta-glucuronidase annotation of regorafenib-associated taxa.csv`

**Title:** β-glucuronidase annotation of regorafenib-associated taxa.

**Description:** Functional annotation of top-ranked regorafenib-concordant and regorafenib-opposing microbial genera for β-glucuronidase capacity. Evidence was evaluated using UniProt EC 3.2.1.31 annotations and, where applicable, published literature. Absence of an annotation should not be interpreted as evidence that β-glucuronidase capacity is absent.

## Supplementary Table S4

**File:** `Table S4 - Overlap of top-ranked baseline and longitudinal metabolites.csv`

**Title:** Overlap of top-ranked regorafenib-concordant metabolites between baseline and longitudinal metabolite profiles.

**Description:** Observed overlap between the top 10, 25, and 50 metabolites ranked by regorafenib transcriptional concordance score in the baseline and longitudinal sensitivity analyses. Expected overlap under random ranking, fold enrichment over expectation, and hypergeometric p-values are reported.

## Supplementary Table S7

**File:** `Table S7 - Concentration-dependent viability effects of framework-prioritized metabolites in CRC cells.csv`

**Title:** Concentration-dependent viability effects of framework-prioritized metabolites in CRC cells.

**Description:** Statistical analysis of HT-29 viability responses to the prioritized metabolites carried forward for quantitative dose-response evaluation. Reported measures include Jonckheere-Terpstra p-values, Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted q-values, Spearman correlations, log-dose regression slopes with 95% confidence intervals, maximal observed inhibition (Emax), and normalized area under the dose-response curve (AUC). Regorafenib was analyzed separately as a positive control. Four technical replicate wells were included at each concentration on the final experimental plate.
